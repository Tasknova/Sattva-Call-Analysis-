# Sattva Call Analysis - Features & HR Agency Benefits

## Overview
Sattva Call Analysis is a comprehensive call management and analysis platform specifically designed for HR and staffing agencies. It combines call recording, AI-powered transcription, and intelligent analysis to provide deep insights into sales calls, client interactions, and employee performance.

---

## Core Features

### 1. **Automated Call Recording & Capture**
- **Exotel Integration**: Seamlessly integrates with Exotel call center platform to capture all inbound and outbound calls
- **Recording Storage**: Calls are automatically stored in AWS S3 for secure, scalable access
- **Call History Tracking**: Complete audit trail of all calls with timestamps and participant details
- **Multi-format Support**: Handles various audio formats for maximum compatibility

**HR Agency Benefit**: Never miss a conversation with candidates or clients. Complete call records provide legal protection and training reference material.

### 2. **AI-Powered Call Analysis**
- **Automated Transcription**: Convert audio to searchable text using AI transcription services
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Detect emotional tone (positive/negative/neutral) during conversations
- **Engagement Scoring**: Measure caller and listener engagement levels
- **Confidence Metrics**: Assess confidence levels in sales pitches and conversations
- **Call Quality Assessment**: Automatic evaluation of call effectiveness and professional standards

**HR Agency Benefit**: Identify training gaps, recognize top performers, and understand candidate satisfaction levels without manual review of every call.

### 3. **Lead & Candidate Management**
- **Lead Database**: Centralized repository for all candidates and client leads
- **Lead Grouping**: Organize leads by campaign, position, or client for better management
- **Lead Lifecycle Tracking**: Monitor leads from initial contact through placement
- **Batch CSV Import**: Upload candidate/lead data in bulk for quick population of the system
- **Lead Status Management**: Track lead progression through stages (contacted, interested, placed, etc.)

**HR Agency Benefit**: Streamline candidate pipelines and ensure no leads fall through the cracks. Better visibility into candidate source effectiveness.

### 4. **Client & Job Management**
- **Client Portal**: Maintain detailed client information and relationships
- **Job Management**: Create and track open positions from multiple clients
- **Client Assignments**: Link leads to specific job requirements and clients
- **Contact Management**: Store client contact information and communication history

**HR Agency Benefit**: Organize complex client relationships and multiple open positions efficiently. Track which placements came from which jobs.

### 5. **Role-Based Dashboards**
The platform provides specialized dashboards for different user roles:

#### **Admin Dashboard**
- System-wide performance metrics
- Employee and manager activity monitoring
- Call volume and analysis statistics
- User management and permissions
- System health and analytics

#### **Manager Dashboard**
- Team performance overview
- Employee call quality metrics
- Team productivity statistics
- Performance trends and comparisons
- Employee call history and details

#### **Employee Dashboard**
- Personal call statistics
- Individual performance metrics
- Call analysis feedback
- Recorded calls library
- Performance improvement tracking

**HR Agency Benefit**: Each stakeholder gets relevant insights. Managers can coach employees effectively. Admins get strategic visibility. Employees see their own development data.

### 6. **Advanced Reporting & Analytics**
- **Admin Reports**: Company-wide performance dashboards and KPI tracking
- **Manager Reports**: Team-level performance analysis and benchmarking
- **Employee Reports**: Individual productivity metrics and call quality scores
- **Employee Productivity Page**: Track daily/weekly/monthly call volumes and outcomes
- **Sentiment Trends**: View sentiment patterns over time to identify issues
- **Performance Benchmarking**: Compare employee and team performance

**HR Agency Benefit**: Data-driven decision making. Identify underperforming employees early. Recognize top performers for incentives. Track team goals and metrics.

### 7. **User Management & Authentication**
- **Role-Based Access Control**: Admin, Manager, and Employee roles with specific permissions
- **Secure Login**: Customized authentication for different user types
- **Company Signup**: Multi-tenant support for managing multiple HR agencies
- **Email Confirmation**: Secure account verification process
- **Session Management**: Automatic timeout and logout tracking

**HR Agency Benefit**: Secure access to sensitive candidate and performance data. Role-based controls prevent unauthorized access.

### 8. **Employee Profiles & Performance Tracking**
- **Employee Profiles**: Detailed employee information and role assignments
- **Performance Metrics**: Track individual KPIs and improvement areas
- **Call Quality Scores**: Automated assessment of professionalism and sales skills
- **Historical Analysis**: View performance trends and growth patterns
- **Manager Feedback**: Structured feedback based on call analysis

**HR Agency Benefit**: Build detailed employee profiles. Identify training needs and high-potential employees for promotion or client-facing roles.

### 9. **Phone Integration**
- **Built-in Dialer**: Quick dial functionality for outbound calls
- **Call Logging**: Automatic call details captured during/after calls
- **Quick Access**: One-click calling from lead records

**HR Agency Benefit**: Streamlined workflow without switching between systems. Better call tracking and follow-up management.

### 10. **Data Import & Integration**
- **CSV Upload**: Bulk import candidates, leads, and clients
- **Data Validation**: Ensure data quality during import
- **Flexible Mapping**: Configure import fields to match existing data structure
- **Batch Processing**: Handle large datasets efficiently

**HR Agency Benefit**: Quick onboarding of existing candidate databases. Easy data migration from legacy systems.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: Modern React with TypeScript for reliability
- **Backend**: Supabase PostgreSQL for secure, scalable data storage
- **Cloud Storage**: AWS S3 for secure call recording storage
- **AI Services**: Google Generative AI for transcription and analysis
- **Integration**: Exotel VoIP platform for call capture
- **UI Framework**: Shadcn/ui for consistent, professional interface
